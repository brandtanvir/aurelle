(01)Our role

Aurelle Aviation SA is an air charter broker and is not a direct air carrier. Flights are operated by certified air carriers that hold operational control of each flight.

Aurelle Aviation SA does not operate aircraft itself. Our affiliated operators and our partner carrier, listed below, each hold their own certificate and operational control of the flights they fly.

(03)United States: FAA and DOT

In the United States, on-demand charter flights must be operated by a direct air carrier that holds an FAA certificate with Part 135 operations specifications, or by a foreign air carrier with the required US authority.

The US Department of Transportation’s rules for air charter brokers, at 14 CFR Part 295, require a broker to make clear in its advertising that it is a broker and not a direct air carrier, never to hold itself out as the operator, and to identify the direct air carrier that will operate the flight.

Aurelle meets these rules with the statement under Our role, which also appears in our quotes, and by naming the carrier on every quote before you pay. Your charter agreement also sets out, in writing, our role as your agent, the carrier’s identity, and how your payment is held until the flight operates.

(04)Europe, Switzerland and the UK

Commercial flights by operators based in the EU, and in Switzerland under its air transport agreement with the EU, require an Air Operator Certificate issued under EASA rules (Regulation (EU) No 965/2012) by the operator’s national authority. In Switzerland that authority is the Federal Office of Civil Aviation (FOCA).

Since 2021, UK operators hold Air Operator Certificates issued by the UK Civil Aviation Authority under UK law.

We never sell flights on aircraft operated without the required certificate, sometimes called grey or illegal charter, including privately registered aircraft offered for hire.

(05)What “our fleet” means

When this website refers to “our fleet” or “Aurelle aircraft”, it means aircraft managed by Aurelle’s affiliated operators, or available to them under long-term contract, each operated by a certified carrier named on your quote. Aircraft shown on this site illustrate a class; the aircraft for your flight is the one named in your charter agreement.

(06)Other licences

Aurelle Aviation SA is entered in the Geneva commercial register under CHE-318.627.904. Each affiliated operator also holds the operating licence required in its own country, and our insurance covers every flight we arrange, whichever carrier operates it.
