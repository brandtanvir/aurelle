(01)Your settings

Loading your current setting…

Your choice is stored on this device for twelve months, then we ask again. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal and do not ask. You can also change your choice from Cookie settings at the foot of every page.

That is the only item. This website sets no cookies of its own today. Storing your choice is strictly necessary, so it needs no consent.

(03)Optional, only with consent

If we add visit statistics, we will use a privacy-friendly tool hosted in the EU that counts visits without advertising identifiers and discards IP addresses once a visit is counted. It will load only after you choose Allow visit statistics, and stop loading as soon as you withdraw. Anything it stores will be listed above before it goes live.

(04)Other services

To run smoothly, our pages load open-source code libraries from public content networks (cdnjs, unpkg and jsDelivr). Like any web server, they receive your IP address with the request; they set no cookies and learn nothing about your bookings.

Links to WhatsApp, maps or email open those services only when you choose to, under their own privacy policies.

(05)Your browser

You can also clear or block site storage in your browser settings. The site still works if you do; we will simply ask about cookies again on your next visit.

(06)Changes and contact

We will update this page, and ask for your consent again, before adding anything optional. Questions go to privacy@aurelle.example. Our Privacy notice explains how we handle personal information more broadly.
