This notice covers this website, our concierge by phone, email and WhatsApp, and the flights we arrange. It is written to meet the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, whichever applies to you.

(02)What we collect

When you enquire

Your name, email and phone number, the route, dates and number of passengers, any preferences you choose (pets, catering, ground transport) and anything you write in the notes.

When you fly

Each passenger’s name, date of birth, nationality and passport or identity document details, which the operator and border authorities require. Your concierge collects these directly, never through this website.

Sensitive information

Dietary, medical or mobility needs, only if you choose to tell us. We use them for that flight alone, with your explicit consent, and delete them once you have landed.

Payments

Invoices and payment records. Card payments are handled by a PCI DSS certified payment processor; we never see or store full card numbers.

Empty-leg alerts

Your email address and, if you give it, your home airport.

This website

Our servers log your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time, to keep the site secure. Logs are deleted after 30 days. Nothing optional is stored on your device unless you allow it; see our Cookie policy.

From someone else

If an assistant, family office or travel manager books for you, they give us your details. We ask them to show you this notice.

(04)Who we share it with

  • The operator and crew of your flight.
  • Handling agents and private terminals at each airport.
  • Caterers, chauffeurs and hotels, only what they need and only when you ask us to arrange them.
  • Border, customs and immigration authorities, which require advance passenger information by law.
  • Our service providers for cloud hosting, email, client records and telephony, under contract.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and authorities, where the law requires it.

Every recipient is bound by a written contract or a duty of confidentiality. None may use your details for its own marketing.

(05)International transfers

Private flights cross borders, so your details travel with you: to the operator and to the authorities of the countries you fly from, to and over. These transfers are necessary to perform your contract.

For our own service providers, we transfer data only to countries that the Swiss, EU or UK authorities recognise as providing adequate protection, or under standard contractual clauses with the appropriate UK and Swiss addenda. Our core systems are hosted in Switzerland and the EU.

(07)Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete it, where we no longer need it or have no legal duty to keep it;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to a use based on our legitimate interests;
  • send it to you or to another company in a common electronic format;
  • stop using it where you gave consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did before.

Write to privacy@aurelle.example. We reply within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO); in the EU, the authority in the country where you live.

(08)Security

Only the people who handle your trip can see your details. Information is encrypted in transit and at rest, every staff account uses multi-factor sign-in, and access rights are reviewed each quarter.

Your concierge will never ask for passport or payment details in an unexpected message. If in doubt, call the concierge number printed on your charter agreement before replying.

(09)Children

Children fly with a parent or guardian, who gives us their details. This website is not intended for anyone under 16 to use on their own.

(10)Changes to this notice

We will post any update here and change the effective date above. If a change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you directly before it applies.
