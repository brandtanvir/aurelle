(01)Who your contract is with

Aurelle Aviation SA, Chemin de l’Envol 12, 1215 Geneva 15, Switzerland (“Aurelle”, “we”), arranges your flight as your agent. Carriage is performed by the operating carrier named in your charter agreement, whose conditions of carriage also apply.

The operating carrier has operational control of your flight: it is responsible for the aircraft, the crew and the safe conduct of the flight. See our Operator disclosure.

(02)Quotes and confirmation

  • Prices on this website are indicative only.
  • A quote is valid for 24 hours and depends on aircraft availability until confirmed.
  • A flight is confirmed only when you sign the charter agreement and we receive payment as it sets out. Nothing is booked or charged until you approve.
  • Before you pay, we tell you in writing the operating carrier’s name and the aircraft registration.

(03)Price, taxes and extras

Your quote includes the aircraft, crew, fuel, standard catering, and landing and handling fees at the airports in your itinerary.

The following are charged only if they arise, and we tell you first: de-icing; extra flying time caused by re-routing or air traffic control; crew overnights or positioning beyond the quote; special catering; ground transport; in-flight connectivity, at cost; and taxes and duties that apply to your trip, such as US Federal Excise Tax and segment fees, VAT, and UK Air Passenger Duty.

(04)Payment

Charter flights are paid in full at least seven days before departure, or on confirmation if booked later. Membership hours are paid in advance and drawn down at your fixed rate after each flight; unused hours remain yours for 24 months.

Payments are held in a segregated client account with a Swiss bank until your flight has operated, and can be made by bank transfer or card.

(05)Changes and cancellation

If you cancelCharge
More than 14 days before departure10% of the charter price
3 to 14 days before departure50%
Less than 72 hours before, or no-show100%

Changes to time, route or passengers cost nothing when the same aircraft and crew can fly them within the quote. Otherwise we requote, and you decide.

If we or the operator cancel for a reason within our control, you choose between a full refund and a replacement aircraft of the same or a higher class at no extra charge.

(06)Replacement aircraft

If the booked aircraft becomes unavailable, for example because of a technical fault, we will offer an aircraft of the same or a higher class, from an operator that meets our safety standards, at no extra cost. We name the new operator before departure. You may decline and receive a refund of any part of the journey not flown.

(07)Passengers, documents and conduct

  • Each passenger needs a valid passport and any visa, health or entry documents the trip requires. We will tell you what we know is required, but holding them is your responsibility.
  • Passenger names and document details are due at least 24 hours before departure, for security screening and border authorities.
  • The captain may refuse to carry a passenger, divert or end a flight for safety, for example if a passenger is intoxicated or threatening, or does not follow crew instructions. Costs caused by passenger misconduct are charged to the client.
  • Smoking and vaping are not permitted on board.
  • Firearms are carried only with the prior written approval of the operator and the relevant authorities.

(08)Baggage and pets

  • Baggage allowances depend on the aircraft and are stated in your quote.
  • Dangerous goods, such as some lithium batteries, compressed gases and flammable liquids, may be carried only as ICAO and IATA rules allow. Ask your concierge if unsure.
  • Dogs and cats travel in the cabin with the operator’s agreement. You remain responsible for the animal and its documents, which we help arrange. A country may refuse entry if its requirements are not met.

(09)Delays, weather and safety decisions

The captain and the operator decide whether a flight can operate safely. Weather, air traffic control, airport restrictions, strikes and other events outside our control may delay, divert or cancel a flight. When this happens we do everything reasonable to get you to your destination, and refund any part of the journey that cannot be flown.

We are not liable for indirect losses, such as missed meetings or onward travel, except where the law does not allow this to be excluded.

(10)Liability

The operating carrier is liable to passengers for death, injury, delay and damage to baggage under the Montreal Convention 1999 where it applies, generally to international carriage, and for EU carriers under Regulation (EC) No 2027/97 and its UK equivalent, within the limits those rules set.

Aurelle’s liability for its own services under these terms is limited to the price paid for the flight concerned. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that the law does not allow to be limited.

(11)Complaints

Tell your concierge, or write to care@aurelle.example. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and reply in full within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, we will refer it to independent mediation in Geneva, at our cost.

For flights to, from or within the United States, you may also contact the US Department of Transportation’s Office of Aviation Consumer Protection.

(12)Law, jurisdiction and changes

These terms are governed by Swiss law, and the courts of Geneva have jurisdiction. If you are a consumer, this does not remove the protection given to you by the mandatory law of the country where you live.

We may update these terms. The version in force when you sign your charter agreement applies to that flight.
